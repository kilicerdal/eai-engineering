## Review method and qualifications

Claims were checked against institutional sources: botanical collections, museums, university extension and teaching resources, weather and geological agencies, and an original ant-symbiosis research paper. Each entry below links to the supporting source; review dates are also kept in the typed source registry. The questions, explanations, reflections and prompts are original wording. No publisher prose or images were copied.

- Botanical food categories differ from culinary names. Common edible bananas are distinguished from seeded wild relatives.
- Cloud mass is an explicit calculation for a stated volume and liquid-water density, not a universal cloud weight.
- Mountain rankings state their reference: sea level, base-to-summit, or Earth’s center.
- Hail layers describe freezing conditions, not a guaranteed count of trips through a cloud. Pumice may float temporarily; polygonal columns are not always hexagonal.
- The 1974 gum scan is the first supermarket UPC checkout scan, not the first barcode of any kind.
- Wood frogs tolerate partial freezing; the cards do not claim every cell freezes solid. Ant bacterial defenses are scoped to Acromyrmex research.
- Experiments are drawing and reasoning prompts, not instructions to handle animals, heat sealed containers, or look at the Sun.
- Existing IDs, browser storage keys and daily snapshots remain unchanged. Each new card has two valid related discoveries and a Why it matters reflection.
