# Recruiter usability study kit

Status: **not conducted with a human recruiter**. No participant or recruiter feedback was available for the November 19, 2026 review. An expert task walkthrough and browser regression checks are separate evidence; neither is a substitute for human comprehension, preference or time-on-task results.

## Setup

Invite an actual recruiter who reviews software quality/automation candidates. Ask permission to observe and take anonymous notes; recording is optional and needs their agreement. Do not collect employer materials, candidate records or private contact details. Use participant codes and record browser/device. Start at the public homepage in a fresh browser. Tell them the site is being evaluated, not their ability.

Read the scenario without pointing to controls or using the interface's exact link labels. Do not send invitations automatically from this kit.

## Tasks

1. You have two minutes to decide whether this candidate deserves a technical conversation for an API/database testing role. Find one relevant example and explain why you chose it.
2. Explain what the candidate claims to have contributed, including the role of AI assistance. What would you still want to ask them?
3. Find a concrete failure the tests detected. Explain the expected and observed outcomes, and whether you just ran software or inspected an existing example.
4. Find the original supporting record and its source revision. Can you tell whether the screenshot, walkthrough, website case and repository release describe the same version?
5. Find how you would ask a follow-up question about a role. Stop before sending any message.

## Observation sheet — leave blank until a real session

For each task record: participant code; device/browser; start/end time; unassisted/assisted/incomplete; first destination; wrong turns; pages/scrolling used; participant explanation; confidence (1–5); relevant verbatim comment with permission. Ask what made the decision easy or difficult after the task. Do not treat navigation alone as evidence of comprehension.

For the two-minute task, record whether they selected a relevant example and could substantiate their decision within the allotted time. Record an incomplete outcome honestly; do not replace participant time with automated click timings. A few participants can reveal problems but cannot establish a population conversion rate.

## Physical-device follow-up

On an actual iPhone/Safari and Android/Chrome, check text zoom, portrait/landscape, menu focus, the three project routes, evidence downloads, artwork dialogs, back navigation and contact links. Log device/OS/browser versions and observed issues. Browser emulation does not mark these physical-device tasks complete.

Method references: [NN/g — writing usability tasks](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/better-usability-tasks/), [Playwright browser coverage](https://playwright.dev/docs/browsers).
