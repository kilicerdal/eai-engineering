# V2.3 — A Thousand Reasons to Wonder

Reviewed 2026-11-14. Fifty new records appended to the unchanged 950-record catalog. Original phrasing; each factual claim checked against the linked institutional source. Why it matters and exploration prompts are editorial invitations, not additional sourced claims.

Qualifications: Europa’s interior and crater-erasing processes remain inferential. Lewis manufacture is attributed, and red staining is an early report. Saffron seasonal timing follows the RHS context. Balance cards describe physiology, not diagnosis or treatment. Fountain-clock examples are scoped to fountain clocks. Pendulum periods are approximate; the source’s erroneous Paris demonstration date is not used. Okapi calls are below human hearing; predator-hearing explanations are not asserted. Navajo cards distinguish the designed military code from the living language.
