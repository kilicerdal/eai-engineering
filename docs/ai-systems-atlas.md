# AI Systems Atlas and engineering philosophy

The homepage /#about section replaces the experience-count biography, certification badges and six technology lists with the approved philosophy and conceptual AI architecture. Attribution remains: Founded by Erdal Kilic. The existing Barcelona architectural background is reused; no new image-generation or paid service is involved.

The atlas describes 18 components across intent/policy, knowledge, orchestration, action, assurance and learning, with 20 conceptual relationships. Visitors can highlight retrieval, agent/tool, and evaluation paths and select any component to inspect its role. A resize observer positions vector connectors as text and layout reflow; it disconnects on unmount. Text is selectable HTML, controls are native buttons, and explanations announce through a live region.

This is an illustrative architecture, explicitly labeled as such. It does not claim these components are a deployed eAI backend, demonstrate model performance, execute tools, or call an AI model. The Engineering Studio link leads to the separate projects and actual recorded evidence.
