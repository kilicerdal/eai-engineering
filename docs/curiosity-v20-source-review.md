# Curiosity Room V2.0 — A World Worth Wondering About

Reviewed 2026-11-13. 100 append-only discoveries take the catalog from 650 to 750. Earlier IDs, wording, source links, reading preferences and Saved storage remain unchanged. Sources are institutions, museums, original research teams and public scientific agencies. Factual wording is paraphrased; questions, thought experiments and significance paragraphs are original editorial writing. Related discoveries make conceptual connections without asserting direct historical influence.

## Review qualifications

Animal claims remain species-specific where appropriate: two-toed sloths, satin bowerbirds, Myrmoteras ants, common cuttlefish and Johnsons sea cucumbers. Counts and historical dates are qualified rather than described as universal records. The nautilus spiral is not claimed to be a perfect golden spiral. Mesa Verde kiva use and storage use retain uncertainty. Earliest successful products are distinguished from first-ever inventions. Smithsonian’s precise tinfoil-phonograph object record supplies the technical mechanism. Aflaj claims use the heritage statement, not its dated water-use percentages. Geology cards are descriptive and do not invite collecting protected objects or trying dangerous processes.

Aquarium of the Pacific pages restricted direct fetching; indexed institutional passages supported the archerfish and sea-cucumber cards. Fulgurite’s Roman-language card explicitly attributes NPS’s interpretation. The USGS pillow-lava page is written by a paleomagnetic specialist. Each source contributes no more than 200 words of factual titles, questions and descriptions to the batch. No third-party images are copied for share cards; they use existing local artwork and code-drawn decoration.
