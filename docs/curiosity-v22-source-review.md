## Scope and qualifications

This collection explores biological functions and observed behavior. Engineering analogies are thought prompts, not claims that an organism intentionally designed a solution or directly inspired a particular product. Species and group boundaries are explicit: African penguins, ruby-throated hummingbirds, Eastern yellow-billed hornbills with dwarf mongooses, crested versus New World porcupines, three-banded armadillos, and several distinct kelps. Not all chameleons have prehensile tails. Chameleon color signaling is included, while the source’s oversimplified skin-color mechanism and casque water-collection claim are excluded. Hedgehog self-anointing retains uncertainty. Cheetah maximum-speed and overheating claims, hyena bite-force superlatives, and old conservation population numbers are excluded. Hyrax buoyancy-like footpad analogies are kept descriptive. Manatee bone-density contribution is qualified. Bamboo “stronger than steel” and fixed oxygen-production claims are excluded; hollow-culm universality is not asserted. Baobab taxonomy, exact water capacities, and hollow-trunk water-tank myths are excluded. Cycad photosynthetic timing generalizations and dinosaur dates from the botanical page are excluded; cone warming and insect interaction are species-qualified. The bromeliad review uses the UF/IFAS PDF after the HTML endpoint looped. Seagrasses are distinguished from seaweeds without presenting an outdated family count. Mangrove protection is not absolute flood protection. Kelp taxonomy is simplified to brown algae, avoiding the older page’s kingdom terminology.
