## Facts editorial policy

The 40 proposed categories are an editorial menu, not a requirement to manufacture one example of each. No payment, private relationship, hidden image, restoration or incident is invented merely to fill a category. Some entries are expressly observations or research limits, so 205 entries must not be advertised as 205 independently verified historical facts.
