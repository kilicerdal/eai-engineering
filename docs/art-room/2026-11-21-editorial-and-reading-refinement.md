# Art Room — editorial and reading refinement

Local only. Nothing published. Preview: http://127.0.0.1:3038/study/art

## Scope and accounting

The approved package combines the preceding 12 suggestions with 30 additional review areas. These are **42 areas of refinement**, not 42 new features, services, or claims of a complete scholarly rewrite. Existing biographies, dossiers, comparisons, preservation tools and collection statements already addressed many areas. This pass changes the weak points and verifies retained behavior rather than duplicating those facilities.

Earlier local depth, facts and reading/evidence passes remain present. All 112 painting studies, 21 painters, 100 hosted reproductions, 12 reference-only works and personal-storage formats are retained.

## Concrete additions and fixes

- Every painting essay now makes its central looking question explicit.
- Twenty signature studies gain individual subject/encounter orientations inside the existing title/version disclosure. They connect narrative identification to visible form and retain limits on sitter psychology, original framing and documentary evidence.
- These new passages are included in scholarship search, with exact disclosure anchors.
- Contextual term support expands from six to twelve terms. Paragraphs can link multiple relevant terms and multiple painting titles without nesting links or losing text. Definitions remain optional.
- Whole-image captions now identify title, date, medium, recorded dimensions and collection, then credit and reproduction limitations. Captions link directly to the structured description.
- Fullscreen detail inspection returns focus to the control that opened it. Failed standard reproductions have a retry; markers and fullscreen are unavailable while the image is missing. Failed higher-resolution sources give a status explanation. Zoom boundary buttons communicate unavailable actions.
- Related-work links return to the original connection card. Painting links inside a biography return to the originating paragraph. No personal-storage schema changes are involved.
- Study contents no longer link to a nonexistent facts section on the 92 works without one.
- Research dossier extraction no longer repeats the same trailing reference and review paragraph after every subsection; section-specific sources remain.
- Attributed scholarship is not universally labeled a disagreement or transcript when its evidence is a different kind of account.
- An unavailable comparison selection explains its fallback; an unavailable Art Room page offers routes to paintings and My Art.
- Four displayed quotations have explicit bounded comparison records and transmission notes. Van Gogh’s edited translation was compared using the indexed institutional passage because the direct page serves a JavaScript gate. Pollock, Monet and Barr were compared with readable museum page text.
- The current Magritte date and older museum dates are documented together without inventing a reason for the revision.
- Rechecked 87 selected source URLs. Connection results remain distinct from scholarship. The current snapshot has 43 restricted pages, five insufficient responses and one access screen. Twelve text/extraction differences against the retained snapshot remain queued; repeated successful retrievals cannot silently acknowledge them.

## Research limitation

This is an implementation and bounded editorial review. It is not independent specialist peer review of every historical claim. Some museum pages still restrict automated retrieval. A successful HTTP response, a matching quote, or an unchanged text hash is not approval of every claim on the site. Outstanding source flags remain visible.
