# Curiosity Room V1.4 source review

Reviewed 13 November 2026. Local preview only.

The collection grows from 60 to 150 discoveries. The 90 additions use 56 distinct source pages or documents from research authors, museums, universities, scientific agencies, and the QR code developer. Each addition has a question, concise factual explanation, source link, thought experiment, editorial relevance paragraph, and two curated related discoveries.

## Compatibility

The original 60 complete records remain unchanged and in the same order, guarded by a V1.3 catalog digest in the tests. Existing browser keys and record versions are retained. Computing & AI is an additional topic; the original ten topic names remain unchanged. Existing images are reused. Daily sessions now retain their five identities; legacy same-day sessions can reconstruct their original V1.3 selection, and a new day uses the expanded catalog.

## Content review

Study results are scoped to the actual tasks and populations rather than presented as universal advice. The taxi-driver result is described as an association. Tardigrade survival is qualified by state and conditions. Mercury ice is described using NASA’s qualified wording. QR correction is not presented as guaranteed recovery from every kind of damage. The moth card does not claim that the insect originated the word bug. Lovelace’s card avoids the disputed first-programmer superlative. Why-it-matters text and thought experiments are editorial reflections, not extra research findings.

The source pages were reviewed through web results and page/document reads. Automated HEAD checks found no 404 or 410 responses; several institutions block or rate-limit scripted requests. These transport responses were not used as evidence for claims. The V&A photographic-process page was chosen over a glossary page that omitted its explanation in the fetched view.
