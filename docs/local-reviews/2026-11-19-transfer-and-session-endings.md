## Changes

1. Completed core-question reviews offer an optional fresh example selected only through an authored concept mapping. Candidates exclude all questions in retained attempts, the original session, the reviewed question and the same shared case. A broader-domain match is never silently presented as the same concept. An exhausted or unmapped concept keeps its existing retry/source options.
2. Results distinguish questions answered from concept mastery and exam readiness. The two newer certification paths receive the same activity-versus-mastery explanation. A successful fresh check says only that the concept was applied in that example.
3. Comfortable endings now cover long tests as well as short practice. Finish here shows practiced topics, one condition to revisit and Resume with the official section. Earlier results stay reachable. The two newer paths retain their focused review destination with a clearer Resume label.

The fresh check is unscored and temporary in the open review. It is clearly labeled as not saved. Existing scored retry options remain available for a saved result. This avoids changing scores, saved editions, storage formats or backup formats. The check is not proof of unassisted understanding, durable retention or exam readiness.
