## Data and grading boundaries

The existing 72 authored question IDs, prompts, choices, keys and source explanations remain unchanged. The new explanatory studies are deterministic extensions of those existing question values, not official exam material or model-generated answers. Worked solutions and diagrams are optional; opening them before checking records help, preserving the distinction from independent work. Reading comparisons become available only after checking.

V1 records remain compatible: new work/reflection fields are optional. Checked attempts are immutable; flags may be cleared afterward without rewriting an answer. Moving to the last item cannot finish a session while unchecked items remain. Local records and optional account snapshot round-trips retain the extensions. A backup import permits up to 32 MiB to accommodate extended history; existing account snapshot limits still apply. The small line-selection/missing-step practice inputs are transient self-exercises; durable scratch and submitted reasoning use the existing saved exam record.
