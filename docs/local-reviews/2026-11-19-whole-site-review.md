## Recommended work, in priority order

1. Finish the pending question-by-question editorial pass before expanding the bank. Verify the deciding requirement, correct choice and each distractor against the relevant official section.
2. Extend authored concept mappings across all paths. Show availability honestly; never substitute wording similarity for a proven concept match.
3. Separate source-link health, editorial review and exact-section support in labels. A fresh date must not imply a stronger review than occurred.
4. Resolve overlap triage into intentional linked case questions versus redundant questions. Preserve historic question identities and saved editions.
5. Consolidate answer-review tools into three intentions: understand the answer, check the source, try again. Keep advanced tools reachable inside those groups.
6. Merge Curiosity's wording/source, evidence/source and source-details entries into one coherent evidence drawer. Save, Next and the discovery remain primary.
7. Unify Tutor entry destinations or explain embedded versus dedicated workspaces. Currently the homepage AI Tutor link opens /study#ai-assistant while room navigation opens /study/tutor.
8. Replace repeated site/studio/footer navigation with one predictable navigation pattern, preserving access to every room.
9. Guide visitors through existing room-specific backups from one starting place. Explicitly identify included work and unfinished exports; do not falsely call a single room file a full-site backup.
10. Improve materials' empty state: Add a source and Try a sample first; defer explanation selectors and passage follow-up information until relevant.
11. Audit all 200 books' chapter anchors and restored passage context, including introductions, notes and appendices. Do not rewrite existing reading positions silently.
12. Expand specific comparative book recommendations beyond the current selected pairings; author/title/genre fallback is useful but less distinctive.
13. Curiosity: validate superlatives and qualifiers; distinguish experimental findings, measured facts and historical interpretations; broaden specific cross-topic bridges.
14. Measure and reduce initial JavaScript per route. Lazy-load secondary learning tools and preserve build-budget headroom; do not remove content or artwork.
15. Mobile usability beyond overflow: inspect reading comfort, nested scrolling, thumb access and keyboard visibility while actually completing a task.
16. Use the owner's walkthrough feedback to decide what to simplify. Automated passes do not establish that a new visitor understands the interface.
