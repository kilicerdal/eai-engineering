# Three distinct local follow-up improvements

These are additional to the earlier 72 consolidated change groups. Existing artwork, book texts, saved-record formats and room services are retained. No publication is included.

1. **Art audio lifecycle.** Intentional cancellation does not produce a playback-failure notice. Callbacks from a canceled or superseded utterance cannot mark newer playback Finished or Unavailable. Collapsing the audio introduction stops its utterance and restores stopped controls. The full transcript remains available. Browser speech was stubbed for deterministic lifecycle checks; actual voice-service availability is not established by those checks.
2. **Museum-guide itinerary.** Selected works retain selection order. Visitors can move each stop earlier/later or remove it individually, including when a collection filter hides its checkbox. Printed stop numbers and order match the itinerary. The printed copy warns that collection ownership does not confirm display availability. Supporting research references are labelled as references rather than promises of current display. Selection remains page-local and changes no saved gallery or notes.
3. **Bookmark-name editing.** Unsaved names participate in the existing navigation/writing guard. A dialog close or Escape cannot silently discard a name edit; Save or Cancel resolves it. Cancel restores the saved name. Save trims the name, clears the guard and preserves the bookmark’s ID, book, section, paragraph and original date. Existing concurrent-name conflict checks remain intact.
