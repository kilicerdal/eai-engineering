# V1.7 — Hidden Connections

Reviewed 2026-11-13. Exactly 100 original additions bring the catalog from 350 to 450. Earlier records, source URLs, insights, IDs, storage keys, topics and backgrounds are retained.

## Review approach

Checked claims against heritage custodians, primary research papers and research-team reports, museums, metrology and space agencies, university teaching resources, and manufacturers explaining their own technology. Each card has a linked source and a review date in the typed registry. Wording and reflections are original; no source images were copied.

## Qualifications kept in the cards

- Biomimicry examples distinguish observations, model experiments, prototypes, and flight tests. No blanket efficiency promises. The whale result is a 2004 idealized-model stall-angle measurement.
- Termite airflow claims are scoped to the mounds measured in the cited study, not all termite species.
- Aerogel density varies by formulation. The approximate air fraction is not a universal density or a claim to a current world record. Capture tracks vary.
- E-paper image-holding power is distinct from a whole device’s power. Microcapsule claims are scoped to the cited two-particle system.
- MRI uses radiofrequency signals, not ionizing X-rays. fMRI is an indirect physiological measurement, not thought photography. These cards explain physics rather than give medical advice.
- Ancient and medieval heritage is credited to its builders. Modern analogies are prompts, not claims that an ancient design caused a modern invention. Site dates and material evidence are separated from interpretations.
- Draw-and-reason prompts do not ask readers to handle chemicals, alter electrical devices, expose eyes to bright light, or use medical equipment.

## Unread-only Surprise

It never falls back to repeats. An exhausted selection produces a helpful status message without clearing history or filters. Existing Surprise me keeps its prior behavior.
