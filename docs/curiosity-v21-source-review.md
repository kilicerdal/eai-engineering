Reviewed 2026-11-14. 100 append-only discoveries bring the catalog from 750 to 850. The complete first 750 records are preserved. Factual text is paraphrased from the linked institutional, technical, and research sources. Questions, connections, and significance paragraphs are original editorial writing. ## Scope and qualifications

These are short discoveries about mechanisms, materials, behavior, and evidence, not operating instructions. Elevator descriptions are attributed to Otis; no guarantee about an individual installation is implied. Beck’s map is distinguished from geographic mapping. The Forth Bridge is not described as the first steel structure. Plywood surface grades and adhesive classifications are not structural or decay guarantees. The Science Museum rubber article contains unrelated questionable geographic claims; those are excluded. Rubber predates industrialization and no sole-inventor claim is made for its entire history. Memory facts distinguish volatile SRAM from refresh-dependent DRAM. Crow findings are species- and study-specific; five years describes the tracked spread of a response, not an assertion about every individual’s memory. Raccoon washing folklore and opossum tick-removal claims are excluded. Maple aerodynamic similarity is not a claim of direct historical influence. Wrinkle findings are a defined experiment and support, rather than prove, an evolutionary hypothesis; the original paper’s indexed abstract supplied the wet/dry result when the full-text endpoint presented a challenge. Maillard chemistry is distinguished from caramelization and simplified without presenting one temperature as universal. Related discoveries are editorial connections.
