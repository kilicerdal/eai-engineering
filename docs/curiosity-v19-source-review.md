# Curiosity Room V1.9 — Small Things, Big Surprises

Local preview, reviewed 2026-11-13. Adds 100 discoveries to the existing 550; all earlier fact records remain byte-for-byte identical. Questions, thought experiments and significance paragraphs are original editorial writing. Each factual claim below links to its supporting institutional, research or museum source. Related links connect distinct cards; editorial analogies are not claims of historical causation.

## Review scope

Checked source pages or their indexed primary-source passages, with species, experimental and historical qualifications retained. The Geneseo diatom and Whipple microscope pages restricted direct fetching; their indexed institutional text was available. Cornell’s owl poster supports both hearing and eye anatomy; the close-use microscope card cites the Science Museum object record. Seed treatment is species-dependent, gecko grip is intermolecular rather than suction, the mantis-shrimp finding is a particular behavioral study, and dated printing examples do not establish the invention of printing. Avoided duplicating the earlier Velcro, zipper, ballpoint and spider-balloon discoveries. No source text or images were copied wholesale.

## New controls

Visit history uses separate versioned catalog metadata and a fixed tab-session baseline. With no previous tracked catalog, the button shows the latest 100 and explicitly explains that tracking starts here; it does not pretend to know the reader’s earlier visits. A later tab session compares known stable IDs against the current catalog. Refresh retains the selection. An older cached tab cannot remove newer recorded IDs. Disabled storage falls back with a notice. Save, Resume, reading history and preferences keep their existing keys.

Report a concern is available on every card. It prepares an encoded email to the existing support address, or copies a report containing the discovery ID, claim, source and optional bounded reader note. Nothing is automatically transmitted or claimed as received. The form resets when changing cards and keyboard/wheel events within it do not trigger discovery navigation.
