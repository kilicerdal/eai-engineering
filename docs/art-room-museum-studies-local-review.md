# Art Room: museum studies and study tools — local review

Date: 2026-11-20. Local-only batch following cc732cd5. Not published.

## Approved improvement mapping

1. Five signature studies: Mona Lisa, Last Supper, Night Watch, Girl with a Pearl Earring and Starry Night; each now exceeds 1,000 words.
2. Consistent editorial structure: retained identification, history, visual analysis and institutional references, with research panels following a shared format.
3. Claim-level citations: selected claims in the five signature evidence ledgers link directly to institutional sources.
4. Known/uncertain distinction: ledgers distinguish documentation, interpretation and limits.
5. Verified quotations: short quotations are attributed to the institutional publication through which their wording was checked.
6. Human biographies: all 15 painters gain a work-specific discussion of working circumstances.
7. Workshops/collaboration: biographies and relevant research distinguish individual attribution from collaborative production and copying.
8. Ownership history: selected provenance stages, sources and gaps for the five signature works.
9. Original placement: conceptual room/placement diagrams, explicitly not measured reconstructions.
10. Audio: optional browser speech synthesis with complete transcript and pause/resume/stop. This is a synthetic browser voice; processing depends on the browser's speech service.
11. Pan/zoom: full-image fit, drag, pinch and keyboard scroll, bounded magnification and reset.
12. Passage/detail links: selected passages open their coordinates in the viewer.
13. Whole-image handling: fit respects original portrait/landscape ratios without stretching or cropping the initial view.
14. Physical scale: shared scale for works with retained dimensional metadata; disabled when unavailable.
15. Composition: five distinct editorial overlays with explanatory legends, not technical tracings.
16. Color relations: illustrative swatches and visual roles for five signature paintings; not sampled pigment measurements.
17. Technique: three-stage making explanations for the five signature works.
18. Conservation: official institutional conservation resources and explanatory context. No new in-app photographic before/after slider or rehosted technical scan is claimed.
19. Preparatory studies/versions: institutional links, with scope and attribution caveats. Newly licensed comparative image files have not been obtained or rehosted.
20. Shared subjects: three four-work exhibitions compare power, interiors and modern life across artists.
21. Focused comparison: question lenses, synchronized detail magnification/position and shared physical scale.
22. Historical relationships: selected before/after works with links and explicit caution against treating chronology alone as proof of influence.
23. Small exhibitions: three thesis-led sequences with four works each.
24. Tour conclusions: conclusions added to the retained tours; exhibition conclusions invite reflection.
25. Reception: distinguish historical/institutional voices, quotation and contemporary editorial interpretation in signature panels.
26. Before/after observations: private, manually saved journals alongside existing painting notes.
27. Detail notes: up to three bounded coordinate notes per painting, with keyboard alternatives and subject notices.
28. Export: collection-filtered private notebook, readable Markdown with references and print/save-as-PDF styling. Existing restorable backup includes journals and detail notes.
29. Visual descriptions: full structured descriptions for the five signature works; the other 95 retain identification and looking guides, not a claimed complete descriptive rewrite.
30. Collection review: automated structural/source/rights/metadata audit of all 100, plus selected primary-source research. This is not independent specialist peer review of every historical assertion.
