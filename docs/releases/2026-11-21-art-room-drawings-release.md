## Preservation and limits

Original catalog IDs, saved-work formats and keys, notes, notebooks, collections, reading positions, backgrounds and the existing homepage opening are retained. No database migration, environment-variable change, account-permission change, retention change or old-deployment deletion is included. Private repository access remains unchanged.

Image credit and rights are per object. Fifteen painting records remain reference-only. Automated source-change/access flags are retained; ten readable changed references received bounded current-page checks and three remain access-limited. Availability is not scholarly authentication or proof of an attribution change.

A main push and successful local checks alone do not establish successful publication. Vercel deployment status and live route/image/browser verification are checked after the push and reported in the release handoff.
