# Curiosity Room V1.5 — Sounds Made Up

Reviewed 2026-11-13. Local preview only. 100 additions; 250 total.

## Editorial approach

Original summaries, questions, reflections and thought experiments. Publisher trivia lists were idea leads, not imported text. Sources below were reviewed through their pages or indexed primary-source excerpts when bot protection blocked direct access. Each claim is scoped to the supporting evidence. Existing 150 records and insights remain unchanged. No source photographs were imported.

## Growth toward thousands

Append future versioned batches using permanent IDs and a shared source registry. Record review date, verify the precise claim, check existing topics for semantic duplicates, and include two deliberate related links before adding a card. Do not rename existing IDs or storage keys. Recheck time-sensitive status claims before release. A CMS and server-side pagination can be evaluated when measured bundle size or editorial workflow warrants them; they are not required for this batch.

## Qualifications

- Polar bear swimming is an observed capability, not a daily norm; ostrich speed is a short sprint.
- Ocean oxygen is gross production, with substantial marine consumption, not a net annual addition or direct breath-by-breath claim.
- The underwater waterfall is a broad dense-water overflow, not a free-falling curtain.
- Pluto timings and solar-system dimensions are approximate. Comet tails require activity; not all comets always display tails.
- Lightning comparison is to the visible solar surface, not the core; no unsafe experiment is suggested.
- Wild arabica conservation status refers to wild populations, not availability of cultivated coffee.
- Horseshoe-crab testing is historical/current use, not a claim that it is the only testing method.
- Snow symmetry describes crystal structure, not a promise that every falling flake is a perfect six-pointed star.
- Why it matters and thought experiments are editorial invitations, not extra source findings.
