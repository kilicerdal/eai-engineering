# Five-minute project tour

The public route is `/engineering/tour`. Suggested reading time is five minutes, split across five stops: overview (30 seconds), checkout retries (90), claim redelivery (90), settlement controls (60), and next discussion (30). These are reading suggestions, not measured completion times or an auto-advancing timer.

Each project stop connects a business promise to a design decision, a dated evidence record, a case study, and its limits. Fragments such as `/engineering/tour#checkout` are shareable and survive reload, Back and Forward. No account or browser storage is required. The tour does not execute repository services or call a model.

Ownership panels on the three flagship cards and case studies distinguish portfolio direction from AI-assisted implementation, test development and documentation. They describe inspectable repository work without attributing every line or design decision solely to a person. They make no employer-delivery, production financial/healthcare, clinical, or exhaustive defect-coverage claim.
