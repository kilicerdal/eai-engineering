# Leonardo da Vinci: painting as an investigation

> Leonardo made painting answer questions that also occupied observation, drawing and invention. His portrait and refectory commissions demanded different negotiations between a convincing body, a coherent space and the unstable relation between an outward gesture and an inward state.

## A workshop education | history | 1

Born near Vinci in 1452, Leonardo entered an artistic culture in which painting, sculpture, metalwork and practical design were closely connected. His training in Andrea del Verrocchio’s Florentine workshop provided a material education as well as an artistic one. Drawing could describe an observed body, prepare a composition or investigate a mechanical problem. The modern division between artistic imagination and technical inquiry is therefore a poor starting point for understanding his career.

His notebooks reveal an exceptionally persistent habit of asking how things work. Yet it would be misleading to treat every painted face as an illustration of a scientific discovery. A painting was also a commissioned object, made within negotiations about cost, delivery, religious meaning and a patron’s expectations. Leonardo’s investigations enriched that work while sometimes competing with the practical obligation to finish it.

## Courts, commissions and unfinished ambitions | history | 1,2

Milan offered a different setting from Florence: a court in which engineering proposals, ceremonial projects and religious commissions could belong to one professional life. Leonardo sought employment with the Sforza rulers and worked on ambitious undertakings, including The Last Supper. His surviving paintings represent only one part of that activity. Unfinished projects and disagreements over commissions are not incidental anecdotes; they reveal the tension between sustained experimentation and the demands of patronage.

His later movements between Italian centers and France also complicate the image of a solitary, independent genius. Assistants, patrons and political upheaval shaped what could be undertaken. Studying the career through these conditions gives his intellectual range more substance than merely listing the disciplines he practiced.

## How a Leonardo establishes presence | analysis |

In the portraits represented here, presence emerges through relationships rather than decorative detail alone. A turn of the shoulders gives a face an answering direction; hands steady a composition; a distant landscape makes the figure seem both close and embedded in a larger world. Edges can soften until a boundary must be reconstructed by the viewer. This is an active encounter with perception, not simply a soft-focus effect.

The same attentiveness operates differently in a narrative scene. In The Last Supper, gestures distribute a response across a group. The arrangement makes individual reactions legible without dissolving the shared event. Compare that outward movement of attention with the reserve of Ginevra de’ Benci: Leonardo’s achievement is not one repeated expression but the adjustment of pictorial means to distinct human situations.

## Materials, survival and reputation | technical | 2,3

The Last Supper cannot be understood as though it were an oil portrait enlarged onto a wall. Its experimental wall-painting technique and subsequent deterioration make material history essential to interpretation. Restoration and photographic reproduction affect what a present-day audience can see. The surviving surface is evidence of both artistic decisions and a long afterlife.

His reputation has also encouraged exaggerated claims, speculative identifications and the search for newly discovered masterpieces. Careful distinctions between autograph work, workshop participation, copies and related versions protect the particularity of the paintings. The Louvre and London Virgins of the Rocks, for example, require separate records rather than interchangeable illustrations.

## A route through the collection | interpretation | 1,3

Begin with the Annunciation to examine a young painter’s organization of a sacred encounter. Move to Lady with an Ermine, where the figure’s turn makes a portrait feel responsive, and then to the Mona Lisa, where firm compositional structure coexists with uncertain edges. Read these changes as different solutions to representation rather than an inevitable march toward a single famous image.

Leonardo’s continuing significance is most persuasive when grounded in these decisions: observation becomes a means of constructing an image, and an image becomes a means of testing how we see. His work rewards an audience willing to distinguish documented history, visible evidence and the interpretations that connect them.

## Ambition across different kinds of work | interpretation |

Compare the finished reserve of Mona Lisa with the collective disturbance of The Last Supper. The first concentrates response in a face whose expression remains difficult to fix; the second distributes response across many bodies. Both depend on the viewer reading differences within a stable arrangement. This is a more precise account of Leonardo’s range than calling every work mysterious.

His identity as an investigator should not make the paintings seem secondary to notebooks or inventions. A portrait, a religious commission and a study of natural forms pose different demands. Their connections emerge through sustained observation and a willingness to test how an image produces knowledge. The unfinished and damaged works also complicate a career narrated only through triumphant masterpieces: ambition, material choice and the obligations of a commission do not always resolve into the same result.

## Working circumstances and the evidence of pictures | analysis |

Compare the tightly organized Annunciation with the turned body of Lady with an Ermine and the different demands of The Last Supper. A career spans an independent panel, a portrait and a wall bound to a room. Drawings and workshop versions can illuminate working decisions, but they must be tied to particular objects. A workshop copy is not automatically an autograph preparatory study; a famous name does not mean that every surviving related image was made by one hand.
