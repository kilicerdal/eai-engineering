# Toward abstraction: a different order of meaning
> Abstraction did not remove thought from painting. It relocated much of the work of meaning into relations among colors, shapes, intervals and material marks.

## More than one origin | history | 1,2
Kandinsky was one important participant in the development of abstraction, not its sole inventor. Different artistic environments produced overlapping experiments. His career connected Russia, Germany and France, and his work changed across those contexts.

The early landscapes, prewar compositions and geometric Bauhaus paintings should therefore not be treated as one unchanging style. Each organizes the relation between recognizable references and independent visual structures differently.
## A painting need not conceal a code | analysis |
Viewers often respond to abstraction by trying to name objects in every shape. Associations can be useful, but they are not the only route to precision. One can describe how a diagonal interrupts a field, how a dark circle changes the weight of a corner, or how overlapping colors imply depth.

Composition VII and Composition VIII make this distinction clear. The former creates a dense orchestration of currents and collisions; the latter gives more individual shapes room to act. Both have structure, but their structures produce different experiences.
## Theory, teaching and the studio | history | 1,2
Kandinsky’s writings and teaching sought connections among color, form and expressive effect. Musical analogies offered a way to imagine painting that did not depend on a represented story. Such theories belonged to their historical circumstances and should not be presented as universally proven psychological laws.

The paintings are more specific than a summary of the theories. Scale, placement and neighboring elements alter what a color or shape does. An encounter with the work can therefore test and complicate the artist’s stated ideas.
## How to look with rigor | interpretation |
Begin with relationships that can be pointed out. Distinguish personal associations from a claim about historical intention. Change viewing distance and notice which structures become legible at each scale.

Abstraction permits openness, but openness need not become vagueness. Its intellectual richness appears when the viewer can explain how an experience is produced, following exact formal decisions without insisting that every painting tell a conventional story.
