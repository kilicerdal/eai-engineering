## Scale is part of the argument | analysis | 1 | section-1

A large field of paint is not simply a small picture enlarged. The viewer can encounter a network at close range and then move back to consider its broader organization. Some of the work occurs in that change of position. A digital reproduction can assist local comparison while acknowledging that neither its dimensions nor its light reproduce the full physical situation of the original.

## Between an event and its surviving trace | analysis | 1 | section-2

A poured line records a material event, but not the whole event. Gravity, viscosity, distance, the support and the painter’s changing decisions intervene in its formation. The surviving mark therefore invites a reconstruction with limits. Critical language that emphasizes action and critical language that emphasizes visual organization can illuminate different aspects of the same surface without becoming interchangeable explanations.

## Do not let a mythology replace a painting | analysis | 1 | section-3

The image of an isolated heroic painter can obscure professional relationships, precedents and the institutions that made the work visible. The Met connects Autumn Rhythm with the mural practices of the 1930s, complicating a story of invention without history. Begin instead with a specific interval, a crossing or a boundary. Ask how those visible relationships support an experience before treating expressiveness as an explanation that needs no further evidence.
