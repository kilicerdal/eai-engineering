## An exhibition culture, not one recipe | history | 1,2
The independent exhibitions beginning in 1874 created an alternative to established institutions. The participants did not share identical methods or social experiences. Monet’s harbor and railway subjects, Morisot’s interiors and outings, and Cassatt’s figures all belong to modern life, but they represent different access to it.

The movement’s name should not make every painting an unplanned outdoor impression. Studio decisions, repeated subjects and deliberate composition remained central. The effect of immediacy could be carefully made.
## The partial view | analysis |
Cropping, oblique viewpoints and visible strokes can make a scene feel encountered rather than ceremonially displayed. These choices also expose the image as a constructed surface. A painting can remain legible without resolving every contour to the same degree.

Monet investigates how atmosphere changes the appearance of a place. Morisot often lets edges remain open, giving domestic space a searching vitality. Cassatt can compress depth and use strong patterns to make intimate subjects formally demanding. Their differences matter as much as their shared independence.
## Access, labor and leisure | history | 1,2
Modern recreation depended on transport, money and other people’s work. Domestic scenes belonged to particular structures of class and gender. A welcoming image can therefore be historically specific without losing its visual pleasure.

Women artists were not simply followers permitted into an already complete movement. Morisot and Cassatt helped define its range through professional careers and distinctive artistic decisions. Their subjects expanded the field of serious modern painting.
## A different standard of completeness | interpretation |
Ask what the painter has made exact and what remains open. Follow a stroke that describes both a material surface and the light falling upon it. Consider how a cropped figure changes your position as a viewer.

Impressionism’s lasting contribution is not merely brighter color. It makes the conditions of perception and modern experience active within the work, changing how a painting can be coherent without appearing uniformly finished.
